How Starlink Direct-To-Phone Works

Revolutionary satellite technology that turns your phone into a global communication device

1

Space-Based Cell Towers

Each Starlink satellite is equipped with an advanced eNodeB modem that acts like a cell tower in space, orbiting at just 550km above Earth - much closer than traditional satellites.

2

Direct Phone Connection

Your existing smartphone connects directly to satellites without any special hardware, apps, or pointing at the sky. It works with any LTE-compatible device you already own.

3

Seamless Network Integration

Satellites integrate with mobile networks like a standard roaming partner, automatically connecting and reconnecting as they move across the sky for uninterrupted service.

4

Global Coverage

The constellation provides connectivity in remote regions where traditional cell towers don't exist, eliminating mobile dead zones worldwide with near-universal coverage.

No Ground Infrastructure Required

Traditional cellular networks rely on towers and cables. Starlink Direct-to-Cell eliminates this dependency by beaming connectivity directly from space to your phone.
