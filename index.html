    How it works

    Satellites in space. Signal in your pocket.

    Revolutionary technology that turns your phone into a global communication device.

    1

    Space-Based Cell Towers

    Each satellite carries an advanced eNodeB modem acting like a cell tower in orbit — just 550 km above Earth.

    2

    Direct Phone Connection

    Your existing smartphone connects directly to satellites. No special hardware, apps, or pointing at the sky.

    3

    Seamless Integration

    Satellites integrate with mobile networks like a roaming partner — auto-connecting as they pass overhead.

    4

    Global Coverage

    Connectivity in regions where cell towers don't exist, eliminating dead zones worldwide.

    No Ground Infrastructure Required

    Traditional cellular networks rely on towers and cables. Starlink Direct-to-Cell eliminates this dependency by beaming connectivity directly from space to your phone — no router, modem, satellite dish, or kit required.

    Do I need a Starlink dish or router?

    No. Direct-to-Phone connects straight to overhead satellites using your existing 4G/LTE smartphone and SIM card.

    How fast does activation happen after paying via M-Pesa?

    Once you pay to Till 3153265 (Jzon Solutions), your data package is queued and activates automatically within 5 minutes.

    Can I share data via hotspot with another device?

    Yes. Once active on your phone, you can turn on mobile hotspot to browse on your laptop, tablet, or other devices.
